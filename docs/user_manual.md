# EV Fleet Analytics Platform - End User Operational Manual

## Introduction
Welcome to the **Electric Vehicle (EV) Fleet Charging & Battery Health Analytics Platform**. This platform empowers fleet managers, maintenance engineers, and corporate executives to monitor battery health, optimize charging schedules, reduce energy costs, and leverage AI insights.

---

## Navigating the Platform

### 1. Executive Dashboard
- **Goal**: Overview of overall fleet health, active EV counts, total energy consumption, and monthly charging spend.
- **Key Cards**: Total Vehicles, Healthy Batteries (SOH >= 90%), Critical Batteries (SOH < 80%), Average SOH, Total Energy (MWh), Total Cost ($).
- **Actions**: Filter by Fleet ID, Location, or Date Range using the top filter controls.

### 2. Battery Health Page
- **Goal**: Monitor battery degradation rates, identify at-risk batteries, and schedule preventive maintenance.
- **Critical Alert Table**: Inspect vehicles flagged with red badges (`Immediate Replacement Recommended`). Click on any vehicle row to drill down into historical SOH degradation curves.

### 3. Charging Analytics Page
- **Goal**: Analyze charging station usage, peak demand hours, and charger type performance (Ultra Fast DC 350kW vs Level 2 AC).
- **Peak Shifting Strategy**: Review the 24-hour demand profile to shift non-urgent fleet charging away from peak hours (14:00 - 19:00).

### 4. AI Assistant Module
- **Goal**: Interact with fleet telemetry using natural language.
- **Example Queries**:
  - *"Which vehicles need immediate battery replacement?"*
  - *"What is the peak charging hour and cost savings opportunity?"*
  - *"Summarize monthly charging expenses for Fleet-ALPHA."*
